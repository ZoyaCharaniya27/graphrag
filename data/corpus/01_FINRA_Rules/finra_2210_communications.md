# FINRA Rule 2210 - Communications with the Public

FINRA Rule 2210 governs member communications with the public and sorts them into three categories: retail communication, correspondence, and institutional communication. A retail communication is distributed to more than 25 retail investors within any 30 calendar-day period. Communications must be fair and balanced, may not be misleading, and must be approved by a registered principal before use where required. Certain retail communications must be filed with FINRA's Advertising Regulation Department.
