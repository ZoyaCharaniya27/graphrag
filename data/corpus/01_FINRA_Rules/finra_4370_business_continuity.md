# FINRA Rule 4370 - Business Continuity Plans

FINRA Rule 4370 requires each member to create and maintain a written business continuity plan (BCP) identifying procedures relating to an emergency or significant business disruption. The plan must be reasonably designed to enable the member to meet its existing obligations to customers and address the member's existing relationships with other broker-dealers and counterparties. Each member must disclose to customers how its BCP addresses the possibility of a significant business disruption.
