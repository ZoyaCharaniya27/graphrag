# FINRA Rule 2010 - Standards of Commercial Honor

FINRA Rule 2010 requires that a member, in the conduct of its business, shall observe high standards of commercial honor and just and equitable principles of trade. It is the broadest ethical rule in the FINRA rulebook and is frequently cited alongside more specific rules. A violation of another rule is often also treated as a violation of Rule 2010. The rule applies to member firms and their associated persons.
