# FINRA Rule 2124 - Net Transactions with Customers

FINRA Rule 2124 permits a member to execute net transactions with a customer only with the customer's consent. A net transaction is a principal trade in which the member, after having received an order, buys or sells the security and reports to the customer a price that is net of the member's mark-up or mark-down. Firms must obtain the required consent and keep records of it.
