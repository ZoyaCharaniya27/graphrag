# Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)

Under the Bank Secrecy Act, broker-dealers must file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) with FinCEN for transactions that are at least 5,000 dollars and that the firm knows, suspects, or has reason to suspect involve funds from illegal activity, are designed to evade reporting requirements, have no apparent lawful purpose, or involve use of the firm to facilitate criminal activity. A SAR generally must be filed within 30 calendar days of detecting facts that constitute a basis for filing.
