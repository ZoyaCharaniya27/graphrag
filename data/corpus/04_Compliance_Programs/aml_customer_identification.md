# AML Customer Identification Program (CIP)

Under the Bank Secrecy Act and its implementing rules, broker-dealers must maintain a written Customer Identification Program (CIP) as part of their AML program. The CIP must collect, at a minimum, the customer's name, date of birth, address, and an identification number, and must include risk-based procedures to verify identity and to check customers against government lists. Records of the information used to verify identity must be retained.
