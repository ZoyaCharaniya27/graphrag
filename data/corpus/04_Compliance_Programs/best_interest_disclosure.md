# Reg BI Disclosure Obligation in Practice

The Disclosure Obligation of Regulation Best Interest requires a broker-dealer to provide retail customers, in writing and before or at the time of a recommendation, full and fair disclosure of material facts about the scope and terms of the relationship, including the capacity in which the firm is acting, material fees and costs, and the type and scope of services, as well as material limitations on the securities or investment strategies that may be recommended.
