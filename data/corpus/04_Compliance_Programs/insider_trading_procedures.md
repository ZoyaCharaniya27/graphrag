# Insider Trading Prevention Procedures

Firms must maintain written policies and procedures to prevent the misuse of material nonpublic information (MNPI), as required by Section 15(g) of the Securities Exchange Act. Common controls include information barriers (also called ethical walls) that separate departments, restricted lists and watch lists of securities, pre-clearance of employee personal trades, and surveillance of trading around material events. Suspected insider trading should be escalated to the compliance department and may require filing a suspicious activity report.
