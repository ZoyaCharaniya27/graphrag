# Books and Records Requirements

Broker-dealers must make and keep current detailed books and records under SEC Rules 17a-3 and 17a-4 and FINRA Rule 4511. Required records include blotters, ledgers, order tickets, trade confirmations, account records, and communications. Rule 17a-3 specifies what records must be created, and Rule 17a-4 specifies how long they must be retained and in what format.
