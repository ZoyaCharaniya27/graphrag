# Series 7 - General Securities Representative

The Series 7 exam, the General Securities Representative Qualification Examination, licenses a representative to sell a broad range of securities products. Since the exam was restructured, candidates must first pass the Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) exam, and the Series 7 serves as the top-off qualification. The Series 7 covers equity and debt instruments, options, investment company products, and the rules governing their sale.
