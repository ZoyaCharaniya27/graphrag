# SEC Rule 17a-4 - Records Retention

SEC Rule 17a-4 specifies how long broker-dealers must keep various records and in what format. Many records must be retained for at least three years, the first two years in an easily accessible place. Certain records must be kept for six years. Records may be stored on electronic media provided the media preserves the records in a non-rewriteable, non-erasable format, commonly known as WORM (write once, read many).
