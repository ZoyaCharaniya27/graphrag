# SEC Rule 10b-5 - Employment of Manipulative and Deceptive Devices

SEC Rule 10b-5, adopted under Section 10(b) of the Securities Exchange Act, makes it unlawful to employ any device, scheme, or artifice to defraud; to make any untrue statement of a material fact or omit a material fact; or to engage in any act or practice that operates as a fraud or deceit in connection with the purchase or sale of any security. Rule 10b-5 is the principal anti-fraud rule used in insider trading enforcement.
