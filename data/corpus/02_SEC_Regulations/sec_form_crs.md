# SEC Form CRS - Customer/Client Relationship Summary

Form CRS is a relationship summary that broker-dealers and investment advisers must deliver to retail investors. It is limited to two pages for a standalone broker-dealer or investment adviser (four pages for a dual registrant) and must be written in plain English. Form CRS covers the relationships and services the firm offers, fees and costs, conflicts of interest, the required standard of conduct, and whether the firm and its financial professionals have reportable legal or disciplinary history.
