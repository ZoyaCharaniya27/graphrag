# Regulation NMS

Regulation NMS (National Market System) is a set of SEC rules governing US equity market structure. The Order Protection Rule (Rule 611) requires trading centers to avoid trade-throughs by not executing at prices inferior to protected quotations displayed by other trading centers. The Access Rule (Rule 610) addresses access to quotations and caps access fees. The Sub-Penny Rule (Rule 612) generally prohibits quoting in increments smaller than one cent for stocks priced at or above one dollar.
