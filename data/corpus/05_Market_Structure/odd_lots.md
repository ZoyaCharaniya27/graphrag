# Round Lots, Odd Lots, and Mixed Lots

In US equity markets a round lot is traditionally 100 shares. An odd lot is a quantity smaller than a round lot, and a mixed lot is a quantity larger than a round lot that is not a whole multiple of one. Historically odd-lot orders were not reflected in the public quotation, though odd-lot trades are reported to the consolidated tape and odd-lot data has been incorporated into market data feeds.
