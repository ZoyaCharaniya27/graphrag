# Trade Reporting Facilities (TRFs)

Trades executed away from an exchange, such as those in dark pools or internalized by a broker-dealer, are reported to a FINRA Trade Reporting Facility (TRF). The TRF disseminates these over-the-counter trades to the consolidated tape. Firms generally must report such trades as soon as practicable and within the time limits specified by FINRA rules, typically within 10 seconds of execution for most transactions.
