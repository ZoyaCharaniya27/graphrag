# Market-Wide Circuit Breakers

Market-wide circuit breakers halt trading across US equity markets during severe declines to reduce volatility and give investors time to assess information. There are three levels based on the decline in the S&P 500 Index from the prior day's close: Level 1 is a 7 percent decline, Level 2 is a 13 percent decline, and Level 3 is a 20 percent decline. Level 1 and Level 2 halts last 15 minutes if triggered before 3:25 p.m.; a Level 3 halt stops trading for the remainder of the day.
